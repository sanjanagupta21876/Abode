

Brand Vision

A modern, energetic, and user-friendly digital companion for college students. The portal prioritizes clarity, ease of use, and quick access to essential hostel services like room booking, maintenance, and mess management.

Visual Identity

1. Color Palette

The palette is centered around a vibrant "Modern Blue" that feels energetic yet professional, contrasted with clean, airy backgrounds.





Primary: #106EBE(Vibrant Blue) - Used for primary actions, branding, and active states.



Surface: #f7f9fb (Off-white/Blue-gray) - Primary background color to provide a soft, modern feel.



Surface Container: #ffffff (Pure White) - Used for cards and elevated components to create depth.



Accent/Alert: Warm yellows for warnings (e.g., fee status) and soft reds for urgent notices.

2. Typography





Primary Font: Inter



Headlines: Semi-bold to Bold weights for clear hierarchy.



Body: Regular weight with generous line height for readability on mobile devices.



Labels: Medium weight for navigation and metadata.

3. Layout & Spacing





Grid: Fluid mobile grid with standard margins.



Corner Radius: ROUND_TWELVE (12px) - Applied to all cards, buttons, and input fields for a friendly, contemporary aesthetic.



Whitespace: Generous padding within cards and between sections to avoid visual clutter.

Component Patterns

Top Bar





Consistent brand presence with the SRM logo and user profile access.



Integrated notification bell for real-time updates.

Navigation





Bottom Navigation Bar: Fixed at the bottom for easy thumb access. Icons include Dashboard, Booking, Services (Maintenance), Mess, and Profile.

Cards





Action Cards: Direct access to services (Laundry, Gate Pass) with clear icons.



Status Cards: Visual indicators for room occupancy (Available, Occupied, Selected).



Info Cards: Structured information for mess menus and notice boards.

Interaction States





Primary Buttons: Rounded with a subtle drop shadow and clear white text.



Selection: High-contrast blue for selected rooms or active filters.



Status Badges: Pill-shaped with soft background colors for "Pending", "In Progress", and "Resolved".

UX Principles





Clarity First: Most important info (Room No, Mess Menu) is always top-of-mind.



Efficiency: Minimize taps for common tasks like reporting a tap leak or checking fee status.



Feedback: Visual confirmation for selections and clear status tracking for requests.
